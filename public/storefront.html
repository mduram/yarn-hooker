How do I order?

Choose a piece, select a size or other available options, and add it to your bag. Open your bag and continue to secure Shopify checkout to review shipping, taxes, and payment. For custom pieces, contact Remy by email or Instagram.

Can I request a custom piece?

Yes! Get in touch with your idea, colours, and sizing to discuss a custom piece.

What about shipping and care?

Shipping options and costs are shown at checkout. For processing times, delivery questions, or care instructions for a specific piece, contact Remy before ordering.
